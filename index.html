What is this?

We throw recurring parties for interesting young people in Austin. Food and drinks are provided, and there’s no application fee.

Who’s gonna be there?

We read every application to make sure all attendees are genuinely unique people. We want people who have big, unique goals, and a willingness to be different.

What’s the catch?

Reid here (from reidsparty.com). I will be completely honest: I’m/we’re doing this for two reasons. The first and biggest is that I’m looking for a life partner, as are some of my closest friends. We’re relatively successful at a young age (22–23), and it’s proven tough for us to find people that really share our values, beliefs, and are within our age range. Thankfully, parties are a great way to get to know people.

That being said, it’s important to me that people who come to our parties are comfortable and have a good time, so we stack the room with a healthy ratio of men and women. That’s also why it’s application only. I really don’t want to waste anyone’s time with boring or ill-intentioned people.

So if you’re interested, and you think you’d be a fit, please apply.
